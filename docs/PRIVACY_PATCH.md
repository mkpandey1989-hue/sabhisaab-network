"Is website par Google AdSense ke vigyapan dikh sakte hain. Google aur uske partner cookies / vigyapan ID se aapki ruchi ke vigyapan dikha sakte hain. Aap https://adssettings.google.com par personalised ads band kar sakte hain. EEA/UK/Switzerland ke visitors se hum Google-certified consent banner se sahmati lete hain."

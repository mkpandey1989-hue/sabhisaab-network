# AdSense + Revenue plan (v2.5.2) - sirf jaayaz tareeke
Maqsad: kamai = visitors x page per visit x wapas aane wale x page ki rate. Bot jo AUTOMATIC kar sakta hai wo bot kare; jo platform ya Google ka niyam chhoota hai wo kabhi nahi.

## KABHI NAHI (account jaata hai)
Khud ad click / dost se karwana, "ad par click karo" kehna, ad auto-refresh, nakli views-traffic-bot, kharidi hui subscribers/likes, spam comment-DM, ek hi reel bina badlaav 5 platform par, ads ko button/navigation jaisa dikhana, zaroori pages (privacy/terms/contact/disclaimer/404) par ad.

## Phase A - AdSense taiyari (abhi se 16 Nov)
1. Bot: 'adsense haal' likhein -> live jaanch (zaroori pages, robots, ads.txt, privacy line).
2. 20-30 sabse accha page chunein; har tool page par formula + misaal + FAQ + "aakhri jaanch" tareekh; 10 kamzor page par text badhao ya noindex (patla/template page = "low value content").
3. Din mein 10 se zyada naye page nahi (code mein seema hai); naye page ke bajaye purane sudharo.
4. AdSense -> Privacy & messaging -> Certified CMP (EU/UK). India ke liye alag zaroori nahi.
5. 16 Nov: apply. Mana ho to wajah padho, theek karo, 22 Jan 2027 ko dobara.

## Phase B - approval ke baad
1. 'adsense id pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX' -> ✅✅ -> bot site/ads.txt chadhata hai (audit gate ke saath). AdSense Sites page par ads.txt "Authorized" dikhe.
2. Privacy page ki "abhi koi vigyapan nahi" line badlo + "Aakhri update" tareekh.
3. Ad slot (sirf tool/guide/lekh/jawab pages): (a) tool result ke neeche 1, (b) lambe text ke beech 1, (c) ant mein 1. Tool ke button ke paas nahi. Har slot par min-height reserve (layout shift nahi), mobile-first, lazy-load.
4. 'canary' har deploy ke baad zaroori pages par ad code ho to ❌ batata hai.
5. 2 hafte baad PageSpeed (LCP/CLS) dekho; ad ki wajah se score girna nahi chahiye.

## Phase C - zyada der rokna (retention, sab jaayaz)
- /jawab: har jawab ke neeche 2-3 related free tool (hai), "aaj ke baaki sawal", category archive, share button (WhatsApp, koi data collect nahi).
- Har tool page: 3 related tool + 1 guide ka link; guide se tool par seedha button.
- Browser mein "7 din ka streak" (localStorage, sahmati/privacy mein likho), Home-screen par jodo (PWA) button.
- Telegram/WhatsApp channel par roz ek digest (8 jawab ek message) - alag release.

## Phase D - ad ke alawa kamai (per-visitor zyada)
- Services lead (custom calculator/web tool): sabse tez asli kamai; form mein budget + kaam ka type.
- Digital templates (50/30/20 sheet, SIP tracker, HRA/rent receipt pack, loan amortization): .xlsx, payment link, refund + terms page.
- Affiliate (Labh Lo Deals, product jaise bijli/inverter/paint tools ke paas): har link par "Affiliate link" + video par #ad. Finance mein demat/broker/loan referral nahi jab tak legal salah na ho (SEBI).
- Platform kamai: YouTube YPP (1 ya 2 channel par pehle dhyan; 1,000 subs + 4,000 ghante ya 10M Shorts views/90 din), Telegram 1,000+ subscribers par Ads revenue share, Facebook Stars/Affiliate.

## Traffic kaise badhe (organic)
GSC ke query se page sudharo -> sirf badla URL IndexNow (max 10/din); har video/post ka UTM (YouTube/Pinterest/FB/Telegram par link, Instagram par bio); har hafte 1 lambi video jisme asli insaani awaaz/calculation ho (inauthentic-content se bachav); har platform ke liye alag hook/caption.

## Andaza (guarantee nahi - maan li gayi sankhyaen)
2,000 visitors/mahina: ~₹70-400. 60,000/mahina: ~₹2,000-12,000. 3 lakh/mahina: ~₹10,000-60,000. Asli rate AdSense/country/topic par; pehla payment $100 (wire) par.
